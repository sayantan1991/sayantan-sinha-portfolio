About

I have spent more than eight years teaching Python, machine learning and data analytics through projects instead of slides. My work sits where classroom learning meets the skills employers ask for: mentoring student AI projects, running workshops and faculty development programmes (FDPs), and turning complex ideas into something a first-time learner can build.

My doctoral research in electrical engineering focused on power-system control, optimisation and intelligent controllers. That background shows up in how I approach AI: measure, adjust, repeat.
